## Synthesis

**(a) Most common ladder.** The numeric convention repeated across the most independent threads: PSL is a face-only scale capped at 8, 4 = median, each whole point = one standard deviation (4 → 50 %, 5 → ~84 %, 6 → ~97.3–97.75 %, 7 → ~99.87 %, 8 → ~99.997 %). Tier names on that 8-point axis are less stable (sub5/truecel ≤ 3; LTN ~3–4; MTN ~4–4.5; HTN ~4.5–5.5; chadlite ~5.5–6; chad 6+; gigachad/"Adam" 7+). A second, equally common ladder lives on a /10 axis and pins tiers to whole digits (LTN 4.x, MTN 5.x, HTN 6.x, chadlite 7.x, chad 8.x, PSL god/gigachad 9+); many users mix the two.

**(b) Differing versions.** Start at 0 vs 1; cap at 8 vs ladders reaching 9–10; 4 PSL = LTN vs 4 = MTN/average; 5 PSL = HTN vs chadlite; normal vs skewed percentiles; "dead average" at 4 vs 4.5; conversions from /10: −2, −3, ×0.8, "2log"; population claims "majority 3.5–4.5" vs "65 % LTN".

**(c) Percentile claims and reliability.** The SD table is purely theoretical (the normal CDF at 1–4 SD) and contested inside the forum itself ("no consensus", "no set criteria for each level", "not normally distributed"). Treat all percentiles as nominal labels, not measurements.

**(d) Explicit rating-guide rules found:** rate the face only (height/body → SMV/IRL); looks-only; neutral expression, full hairline, frontal + Frankfurt-plane side + ¾ views; camera at eye level ~6–8 ft, no zoom, no filters; discount "frauding"; criteria = harmony, balance, symmetry, sexual dimorphism, averageness, unique qualities; quarter-point granularity; no 9/10 on the PSL axis; "if borderline, choose the lower tier"; optional front 60 % / side 40 % weighting (single opinion). No explicit "ignore grooming" rule surfaced. PSL vs normie: coined to distinguish from "normie inflated ratings"; offset 2–3 points below a casual /10; no Tinder-specific mapping exists.
