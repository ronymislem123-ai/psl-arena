# Raw report: direct-verification pass (looksmax.org / incels.wiki / PubMed / PMC, 2026-11-06)

Produced by a research sub-task after the development environment's network allow-list had been extended to looksmax.org, incels.wiki, pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov and doi.org.

## Access summary
- **looksmax.org: blocked by the site, not by the proxy.** TLS succeeds, then every URL (even `/robots.txt`) returns HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare managed JavaScript challenge ("Just a moment… Enable JavaScript and cookies to continue"). No post text, date or author is visible. No attempt was made to circumvent the bot check. `web.archive.org` and `archive.ph` are refused by the environment's network policy, so archived copies could not be tried. **None of the 11 looksmax threads could be verified from the page.** For each, only search-engine snippet evidence exists (labelled as such).
- **pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov: reachable but gated** (JavaScript cookie gate / reCAPTCHA on search). Not bypassed.
- **pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov: fully readable** (search and full text). **doi.org** resolves, but most publisher targets are outside the allow-list. **incels.wiki: fully readable.**

It claims to measure attractiveness on a more normal distribution than the Decile chart"; "'PSL' itself is not a scale, PSL is the community from which this scale comes from"; "PSL rating saw 0/10 as the minimum, 4/10 as average, and 8/10 as the maximum, in modern blackpill circles 1/10 is the minimum, 5/10 is average, and 9/10 is the maximum"; "the population variance in attractiveness for PSL ratings is generally not clearly defined or based on empirically rank-ordered attractiveness ratings". No population averages and no 5–9° range on the page.

## Papers
- **Legan & Burstone 1980** — PubMed gated; not in PMC. Secondary corroboration: PMC4444660 cites "Legan HL, Burstone CJ. Soft tissue cephalometric analysis for orthognathic surgery. J Oral Surg. 1980;38:744–751" and tabulates the Caucasian norms "Facial convexity angle G–Sn–Pg′ 12 ± 4" and "Nasolabial angle Cm–Sn–Ls 102 ± 8".
- **Farkas et al. 1985** — PubMed gated; not in PMC. PMC4369102 confirms the citation ("Plast Reconstr Surg. 1985;75:328–38", DOI 10.1097/00006534-198503000-00005) and states that anthropometric findings "indicated that the neoclassical facial canons were not valid". How many canons held in 1985: unverified.
- **Rhodes 2006** — not retrievable (PubMed gated, publisher outside allow-list); a citing paper (PMC11828934) calls it a "meta-analytic review" of symmetry effects without numbers. Effect sizes: unverified.
- **Ward, Ward & Paskhover 2018** — **verified** (PMC5876805, full text): "When taken at 12 in away and keeping the bizygomatic breadth constant, selfies increase nasal size by 30% in males and 29% in females compared with an orthographic projection"; DOI 10.1001/jamafacial.2018.0009.
