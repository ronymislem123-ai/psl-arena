## Observations

- **Convention vs. value.** Which metrics are used is highly consistent (FWHR, ESR, midface, canthal tilt, thirds/fifths, bigonial:bizygomatic, chin:philtrum, mouth:nose, eye aspect ratio, jaw frontal angle, gonial angle, convexity). The exact ideal bands differ by 2–10 % between threads, and landmark definitions differ, so any implementation must state its landmarks and use bands, not points.
- **Frontal-only reality.** The community itself says projection (maxilla, chin, nose), gonial angle, ramus length, cervicomental angle and convexity are profile-only; the ogee curve is judged in ¾ view and is lighting-dependent. Posters admit photos are distorted by focal length/distance and that mm targets vary with skull size.
- **Dubious / unmeasurable claims to exclude:** absolute mm targets; 2D gonial angle as a precise number; "chin should overshoot glabella"; 5:1 chin:philtrum; 1.35 bizygomatic:bigonial; spurious precision such as canthal tilt "7–7.3°"; "horizontal scleral show is good"; percentage weightings that are personal estimates; several "ratio" threads are AI-generated estimates.
- **Recommendation:** adopt the Full Measuring Guide frontal table with tiered bands, label profile metrics as "requires side photo", present harmony weighting as user-configurable, and re-verify every number against the live threads once looksmax.org is reachable.
